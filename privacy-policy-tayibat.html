1About the app

Taybaat – نظام الطيبات للدكتور العوضي ("Taybaat", "the app") is an Android application that helps you check foods, plan meals, follow a fasting schedule with suhoor and iftar times, and organise a shopping list. It is available in Arabic, Moroccan Darija, English, French and Spanish.

Taybaat is built local-first: everything you create in the app is stored on your own Android device.

Privacy summary Taybaat does not require an account and does not upload your name, meal plans, shopping lists, favourites, history or location to any Taybaat server. We do not operate such a server.

3Location and fasting times New

To show suhoor and iftar times, the app needs to know roughly where you are. You have three options:

  • Choose a city from the built-in list. No location permission is needed.
  • Use my location. Only if you tap this option, Android asks you to allow approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION). Taybaat never requests precise (GPS-level) location.
  • Do nothing. The app estimates a nearby city from your device's time zone, without using any location permission.

When you use your location, the app reads your approximate position once, rounds the coordinates to about 0.01° (roughly one kilometre), and stores them on your device together with the nearest city name. Suhoor and iftar times are then calculated entirely on your device using an astronomical formula; no location or time request is sent to any server.

Location is never used for advertising, never shared, and never collected in the background. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Android settings, switch to a chosen city in the app, or delete it with the app's data-deletion feature (see section 11).

4Notifications

Taybaat can show local notifications that you turn on yourself: meal reminders, and reminders before the end of suhoor and before iftar on your fasting days. These reminders are scheduled on your device by Android; no notification service or server is involved.

On Android versions that require it, the app asks for notification permission first. You can deny it or turn notifications off at any time; the rest of the app keeps working.

5Built-in food database

Taybaat includes a built-in database of foods, their classifications and explanations, used by search, food checks and meal planning. It is application content, not personal information, and it ships inside the app. Internal food identifiers are used only to make the app work and do not identify you.

6Advertising (Google AdMob) Updated

From version 1.3, Taybaat is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob:

  • a banner near the end of some screens (Home, Check, Plan, Shopping list);
  • an occasional full-screen (interstitial) ad after you generate a meal plan, at most once every 10 minutes.

Ads are only requested after Google's consent tool (section 7) says it is allowed. Taybaat does not give Google, or anyone else, your name, meal plans, food lists, history or saved location.

7Consent and privacy choices

Taybaat uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a Google-certified consent tool. When the law where you live requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, or in some US states), a consent message appears when you open the app, before any ad is requested.

  • If you accept, Google may show personalised ads.
  • If you do not accept, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads that do not use your data for personalisation, where permitted.
  • You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy & legal → Privacy options.

Where no consent message is legally required, Google shows ads according to its policies and your Android ad settings.

8Data collection and sharing

Taybaat does not sell or rent your personal information, and does not collect it on any server we control. Your name, plans, lists, history and saved location stay on your device and are never passed to advertisers.

The only data that leaves your device is what Google's Mobile Ads and UMP SDKs collect to show ads and record your consent choices, as described in sections 6 and 7. For Google Play's Data safety section this is declared as data collected and shared for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

9Internet and network access

Taybaat uses internet access only for Google's consent tool and ads. The app does not use the network to upload your data, and suhoor and iftar times are calculated offline, so the rest of the app works without a connection (ads simply do not appear).

10Device backups

If you have turned on Android's backup (for example Google One / Google Drive backup), Android may include the app's local data in your personal device backup and restore it on a new phone, according to your device settings and the app's backup rules. Such backups are stored in your own Google account under Google's terms; Taybaat has no access to them. You can manage or turn off backups in Android settings.

11Deleting your data

The app's privacy screen lets you delete the information you created. This removes:

  • Your saved name
  • Planned meals and planned food entries
  • Meal and food-check history
  • Shopping-list items
  • Favourites and personal food-list entries
  • Location coordinates saved from "Use my location"

The built-in food database, and app preferences such as language, theme and a city you chose from the list, are kept so the app continues to work. They are app settings rather than personal content.

No account to delete Because there is no Taybaat account or server database, there is no separate server-side deletion request to make.

12Uninstalling Taybaat

Uninstalling the app removes its local data from your device, subject to Android's behaviour and any backups you enabled (section 10). Taybaat keeps no server copy of your information.

13Children's privacy

Taybaat is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through any online service. Fasting and diet decisions for children should be made with a parent and a qualified health professional. If you have a concern, contact us using the details below.

14Security

Your data is kept in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. No electronic storage is perfectly secure, so please protect your phone with a screen lock and keep Android up to date.

15Third-party components

  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) – consent management.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK – banner and interstitial ads (section 6).
  • Fonts: IBM Plex Sans Arabic and Noto Naskh Arabic, bundled inside the app under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. They are files only and process no data.

16Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Moroccan law 09-08 or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because Taybaat keeps your information on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app (edit your profile, delete your data) or in Android settings (permissions, backups). For advertising, use the in-app Privacy options and your Android ad settings; for data Google holds, see Google's privacy tools at myaccount.google.com. For anything else, contact us.
