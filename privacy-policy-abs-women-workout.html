Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 06, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to ABS Women Workout (“we,” “our,” or “us”). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application (the “Application”).

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information in the following ways:

a) Personal Data You Provide (Optional)

  • Profile information such as age, weight, and height (stored only on your device)
  • Workout reminders and preferences (stored locally using SharedPreferences)

b) Usage Data (Automatically Collected)

  • Workout progress stored locally (SQLite)
  • App settings stored locally

c) Device Information (via Third-Party Services)

We use Google AdMob and may use Google Firebase. These services may collect:

  • Device type, operating system
  • IP address and advertising ID (for ads)
  • General analytics about app use

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Improve and personalize workouts
  • Display progress and BMI calculations
  • Serve non-personalized or personalized advertisements
  • Analyze usage to improve app performance (if Firebase is enabled)

4. European GDPR Consent

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, we request your consent for personalized ads, in compliance with GDPR and Google’s EU User Consent Policy. You can change or withdraw consent at any time in the app’s privacy settings or device settings.

5. California Consumer Rights (CCPA)

California users have the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell your data; however, personalized advertising may be considered a “sale” under CCPA. You may disable ad personalization in device privacy settings.

6. Sharing of Information

  • With Google AdMob (advertising)
  • With Google Firebase (if analytics are used)
  • To comply with the law

We do not sell or share your profile data stored locally on your device.

7. Third-Party Service Providers

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses AdMob data
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

8. Tracking Technologies

  • Local data stored on your device
  • Advertising identifiers used by AdMob

9. Data Retention

Your workout data remains under your control and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

10. Children’s Privacy

This app is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

11. Security

We implement reasonable measures to safeguard data stored on the device. No system is fully secure.
