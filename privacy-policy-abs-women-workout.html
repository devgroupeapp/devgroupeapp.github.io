Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to ABS Women Workout (“we,” “our,” or “us”). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application (the “Application”).

By using the Application, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Application.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will always reflect the latest version.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information in the following ways:

a) Personal Data You Provide (Optional)

  • Profile Information: If you create a profile, you may provide details such as age, weight, and height for BMI calculation. This data is stored only on your device.
  • Workout Reminders: If you set reminders, we save your preferred time and status locally using SharedPreferences.

b) Usage Data (Automatically Collected)

  • Workout Progress: Stored locally on your device in a SQLite database.
  • App Settings: Sound, rest time, language, and preferences are stored locally using SharedPreferences.

c) Device Information (via Third-Party Services)

We use Google AdMob (advertising) and may use Google Firebase (analytics, notifications). These may automatically collect:

  • Device model & operating system
  • IP address & network info
  • Advertising ID (for ad personalization)

Refer to Google’s Privacy Policy for more details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Personalize your workout experience
  • Calculate BMI and display workout progress
  • Manage reminders and app settings
  • Serve ads through Google AdMob
  • (If enabled) analyze app usage through Firebase Analytics
  • Send push notifications (if allowed)
  • Ensure app functionality and comply with legal obligations

4. Sharing of Information

  • Legal Requirements: To comply with laws or protect rights and safety.
  • Third-Party Services: Google AdMob (ads) and Google Firebase (analytics/notifications).
  • With Your Consent: If you explicitly allow sharing.

Most workout data is stored locally and not transmitted to us.

5. Tracking Technologies

  • Local Storage: SharedPreferences and SQLite store your progress and settings on your device.
  • Third-Party SDKs: Google AdMob may use cookies and advertising identifiers for ad personalization.

6. Data Retention

Your workout progress and settings remain on your device. You can reset or delete data by:

  • Using the “Reset Progress” option in the app
  • Clearing app data in device settings
  • Uninstalling the app

7. Your Privacy Choices

  • Edit or delete your profile at any time
  • Enable/disable reminders
  • Disable notifications via device settings
  • Opt out of personalized ads in your device settings

8. Children’s Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

9. Permissions Requested

  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM – To set workout reminders
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS – To show workout reminders (Android 13+)
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – For displaying ads and connectivity checks
  • VIBRATE – For workout reminders (if enabled)

We only request permissions necessary for app functionality.

10. Links to Other Sites

The Application may contain links (e.g., Google Play Store, privacy policy page). We are not responsible for third-party privacy practices. Please review their policies when visiting external sites.

11. Security of Your Information

We take reasonable security measures to protect data stored locally on your device. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.
