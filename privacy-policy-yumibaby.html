YumiBaby

Privacy Policy

How YumiBaby handles information about you and your baby.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Overview

YumiBaby – Baby Recipes & Feeding Tracker is an Android application that helps parents and caregivers introduce solid food: it shows foods and recipes suited to a baby's age, helps plan meals and batch cooking, and keeps a feeding log.

Everything you enter about your baby stays on your device. YumiBaby does not send your baby's profile, photo, feeding log, meal plan or settings to the developer, to Google or to any other third party.

YumiBaby is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads are non-personalized: they are not based on your interests or on a profile built from your activity.

In short: no account, no analytics, no tracking by the developer. Your baby's information never leaves your phone. The app connects to the internet only to show ads and, where the law requires it, to ask for your consent. Google AdMob receives technical information about your device to show and measure ads, as described in Section 5. Your baby's information is not included in Android backups.

2. Information You Enter

To use YumiBaby, you enter information about your baby:

  • The baby's name or a nickname, date of birth and the feeding program you choose;
  • An optional profile photo;
  • Feeding log entries: the food or milk feed, date and time, meal, and, if you choose, the baby's reaction, a note, and whether you noticed possible symptoms afterwards;
  • Your meal plan, frozen batches, shopping-list ticks and saved foods and recipes;
  • Your preferences: language and usual meal times.

The date of birth is used on your device to show foods, textures and recipes suited to the baby's age. Logged symptoms are used on your device to keep that food out of suggestions and shopping lists. This information is never sent anywhere, and it is never shared with the advertising service.

3. Profile Photo

Adding a photo is optional. You can:

  • Choose a photo with Android's system photo picker. YumiBaby does not request access to your photos: it only receives the one photo you pick.
  • Take a photo with your phone's camera app. YumiBaby does not request the camera permission. The camera app writes the new photo to a temporary file inside YumiBaby, which is deleted straight after use.

YumiBaby keeps only a small, square copy of the photo in its private storage on your device. Your original photo is never changed, and the photo is never uploaded. You can change or remove it at any time on the Profile screen.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

YumiBaby stores the information described in Sections 2 and 3 in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps, including the advertising service, cannot read.

The developer cannot access this information. YumiBaby does not operate a cloud account, server or synchronization service.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

YumiBaby shows banner ads, and a rewarded ad only when you choose to watch one to unlock an extra, using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). YumiBaby requests non-personalized ads only.

What Google AdMob may collect

Even for non-personalized ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects technical information from your device and sends it to Google, including:

  • the device's advertising ID, where available and allowed by your settings;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (such as country or city);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language, screen size and app version;
  • information about the ads shown, such as which ads were displayed or tapped, and diagnostic information about the ad service.

How it is used

Google uses this information to deliver ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure and report on ads in aggregate, and to detect and prevent fraud and abuse. Non-personalized ads are chosen using context, such as the app and your general location, and not from a profile of your interests.

What is never shared

YumiBaby does not give Google or any advertiser your baby's name, date of birth, photo, feeding log, reactions, symptoms, notes, meal plan or any other information you enter.

Your choices

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, YumiBaby asks for your choice through Google's consent form before ads use your device's storage or identifiers. You can change this choice at any time from Privacy choices in the app's Settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your device's advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on your device).

Google processes this information under its own policies: the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The developer does not receive this information, apart from aggregated reports such as the number of ads shown and earnings.

6. Internet Connection and Links

YumiBaby uses the internet only for advertising and for the consent form described in Section 5. All foods, recipes, safety notes and pictures are included in the app itself, and everything except ads works offline.

Tapping an ad may open a website or app run by the advertiser. That website or app has its own privacy policy.

The links to this Privacy Policy and to the Terms and Conditions, in Settings, open in your browser only when you tap them and contain no personal data. Your browser then connects to devgroupeapp.github.io, hosted by GitHub, which receives the usual technical connection details such as your IP address under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Names of public health organizations shown as sources (such as the NHS or CDC) are given for reference only and are not links.

7. Permissions and What We Do Not Collect

YumiBaby's only permissions come from Google's ads library: internet access, network state, access to the advertising ID, Android's ad measurement (attribution) permission, and permissions that let the library finish short background tasks. The interest-based Topics permission is removed, because ads are non-personalized. YumiBaby does not have:

  • account registration or sign-in, or any request for your name, email address or phone number;
  • analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools run by the developer;
  • personalized advertising;
  • permissions for location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

8. Sharing and Disclosure

The developer does not sell, share or transmit the information you enter in YumiBaby to anyone. The only information that leaves your device is the technical information collected by Google AdMob, described in Section 5.

9. Android Device Backup

YumiBaby turns off Android's backup and device-to-device transfer for all of its data. Your baby's information is therefore not copied to Google or any other backup provider, and it will not move to a new phone.

Because there is no backup, uninstalling the app, clearing its data or losing your phone permanently deletes this information. The app reminds you of this on the Settings screen.

10. Data Deletion

You can delete your information at any time:

  • Edit or delete individual log entries, planned meals, frozen batches and saved items in the app;
  • Remove the profile photo on the Profile screen;
  • Use Delete profile on the Profile screen, which removes the baby's name, date of birth, photo, feeding log, meal plan and saved items from the device;
  • Clear the app's data from your Android settings, or uninstall the app, which removes everything the app stored.

The developer never receives your information, so there is nothing to delete on the developer's side. For information held by Google for advertising, reset or delete your advertising ID and use the controls described in the Google Privacy Policy.

11. Children's Privacy

YumiBaby is intended for parents and caregivers aged 18 and over, not for use by children. Ads in the app are directed at adults. Information about a baby is entered by the parent or caregiver and stays on their device. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from or about children, and none of the baby's information is shared with the advertising service.

12. Security

Your baby's information is kept in the app's private storage on your device, and there is no YumiBaby account or server holding it. Ads are loaded over encrypted connections by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

You are responsible for protecting access to your Android device, for example with a screen lock, since anyone who can use your unlocked phone can open the app.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The date at the top of this page shows the current version. If personalized advertising, analytics, backup or any other data collection were ever added, this policy would be updated before that version is released.
