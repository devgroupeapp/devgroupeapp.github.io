Privacy Policy

FocusFlow — Developed by AmineDev

Last Updated: June 10, 2026

1. Introduction

FocusFlow is a productivity and focus timer application developed by AmineDev. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled within the application.

2. Information Stored Locally

FocusFlow stores user preferences, display name, focus sessions, statistics, achievements, language preferences, and settings locally on your device using Android storage technologies such as Room Database and DataStore.

3. Personalization

You may optionally provide a display name during onboarding. This name is stored locally and used only to personalize your experience.

4. Advertising

FocusFlow may display advertisements through Google AdMob. Google may collect Advertising ID, device information, diagnostic information, and ad interaction data according to Google's privacy practices.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Consent Management

The application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent requirements for GDPR, UK GDPR, Switzerland, and applicable privacy regulations.

6. Data Export and Deletion

Users may export productivity data when available. Users may also delete all application data through the app settings. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored data according to Android behavior.

7. Notifications

The application may display local reminders and focus session notifications. Users can disable notifications at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

FocusFlow does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising behavior may be adjusted according to applicable laws and platform requirements.

9. Security

Reasonable security measures are applied, however no storage method can guarantee complete security.

10. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
