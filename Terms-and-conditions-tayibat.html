5Food information and classifications

Taybaat contains a built-in food database. Its classifications (allowed, conditional, forbidden, not yet verified), explanations and frequency guidance are based on the lists of the Tayyibat system as publicly published on altayebaat.com, as understood and organised by the developer. Foods that do not appear on those lists are marked as not yet verified.

Classifications can contain mistakes, may change when the published lists change, and may not reflect every recipe, brand, ingredient, preparation method or regional name. Always read product labels, ingredient lists and allergen information. Food names in Moroccan Darija and other languages are translations provided for convenience.

6Fasting features and prayer times Updated

Taybaat lets you choose a fasting schedule (for example Monday and Thursday, or the white days), shows suhoor and iftar times, counts down to them and can remind you before them.

Suhoor and iftar times are calculated on your device from your chosen city or approximate location, using the calculation method selected automatically for your country or chosen by you. They are estimates. They can differ by a few minutes from the official timetable of your country, your local mosque or announcements, especially near the edge of a time zone, at high latitudes or when daylight-saving rules change.

When in doubt, follow your local official timetable. AmineDev is not responsible for the validity of a fast or of any act of worship based on the times shown in the app.

Reminders depend on Android delivering notifications; battery-saving modes, permissions or device settings can delay or block them. Fasting may be unsuitable for some people; see section 3.

7Location feature New

Using your location is optional. If you choose "Use my location", the app asks Android for approximate location to find your nearest city for the fasting times. You can instead choose a city from the list. Accuracy depends on your device and network, and the nearest listed city may not be your exact town. How location is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.

8Meal planner and shopping list

Generated meals, portions and shopping quantities are automatic suggestions built from the food database and simple rules (for example meal type, food role and how often a food is allowed). They are a starting point you can edit; they are not a nutritional prescription and do not take into account calories, allergies or medical needs.
