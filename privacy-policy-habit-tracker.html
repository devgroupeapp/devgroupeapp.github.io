Privacy Policy

FlowTracker: Habit Planner

Last updated: November 11, 2025

1. About the App

FlowTracker: Habit Planner is a mobile application developed by AmineDev to help users build and track healthy habits. We respect your privacy, collect minimal data, and give you full control over your information.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Local Data (Stored on Your Device Only)

All habit-related information you enter in the app is stored locally on your device. We do not store, transmit, or access your data on external servers.

2.2 Third-Party Services

FlowTracker uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
    • Used to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads.
    • Google may collect certain information such as:
      • Device information and device identifiers
      • Approximate location
      • App interactions
      • Diagnostic information
      • Advertising ID (for ad personalization if consent is given)
    • AdMob processes data according to Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Play Services terms.

3. GDPR, UK GDPR, and Swiss Privacy Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for personalized advertisements at any time
  • Restrict or object to certain data processing
  • File a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain consent where legally required. You can manage or withdraw your consent through the in-app privacy settings.

To make any privacy request, contact us at: devgroupeapp@gmail.com

4. Data Deletion

Since all habit data is stored locally on your device:

  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes your habit data
  • Backups are only kept if you manually export them
  • We do not retain any data on external servers

5. Children’s Privacy

FlowTracker does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years old (or the age of digital consent applicable in your region). If you believe such data was unintentionally collected, please contact us immediately.

6. Security

We apply standard security practices to protect your locally stored data. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

For ads and analytics, third-party services (such as Google AdMob) apply their own security controls.

7. Consent Management (AdMob & UMP)

For users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, the app:

  • Uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent collection
  • Displays a consent form before showing ads where required
  • Allows withdrawal or modification of consent at any time
  • Serves non-personalized ads if consent is denied

Links to Google’s policies (also available within the app):

  • Google AdMob & Partners Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The latest version will always be available inside the app and on our official webpage.
