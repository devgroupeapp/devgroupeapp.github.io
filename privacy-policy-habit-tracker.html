Privacy Policy

FlowTracker: Habit Planner

Last updated: September 12, 2025

1. About the App

FlowTracker: Habit Planner is a mobile application developed by AmineDev to help users build and track healthy habits. We respect your privacy and minimize data collection.

2. What Data We Collect

All user-entered data is stored only on your device. We do not collect or access habit-related data on our servers.

The app integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (for ads) — used to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google may collect:
    • App interaction data
    • Device identifiers
    • Approximate location

3. GDPR & CCPA Rights

If you are located in the EEA or California, you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for ads personalization
  • File a complaint with a Data Protection Authority

To make such a request, please contact: devgroupeapp@gmail.com

4. Data Deletion

Your data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes your habit data unless you exported a backup manually.

5. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have unintentionally collected such data, please contact us immediately.

6. Security

We apply standard security measures to protect your local data and interactions with third-party services. However, no method of data transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The most recent version will always be available within the app and on this page.
