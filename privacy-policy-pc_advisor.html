PC Advisor

Privacy Policy

How PC Advisor handles information and protects your privacy.

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Overview

PC Advisor is an Android application that helps you choose a computer, plan a PC build, compare parts and learn about computer hardware.

Everything you enter is processed on your device. PC Advisor does not send your questionnaire answers, saved recommendations, saved PC builds or settings to the developer or to any third party.

In short: no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking. Your answers, saved recommendations and saved builds stay on your device. The app only downloads public, digitally signed data files from the developer's website, and no personal data is sent with them. Links to other websites open in your browser only after you tap them.

2. Information You Enter

During a consultation, you may select information such as:

  • Device type (laptop or desktop) and intended use;
  • Workloads and software applications;
  • Gaming preferences, where applicable;
  • Budget range;
  • Hardware priorities;
  • Multitasking preferences; and
  • Other choices required by the recommendation questionnaire.

You may also choose parts in the PC Builder and in Compare, and type the specifications of an existing computer into "Check a computer".

These selections are used only on your device, by the app's own rules, to produce recommendations, compatibility checks and comparisons. The specifications you type into "Check a computer" are only shown on screen and are not saved.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

PC Advisor uses local storage for application functionality:

  • Saved recommendations, together with the questionnaire requirements used to produce them, if you choose to save them;
  • Saved PC builds (the parts you chose and the build name), if you choose to save them;
  • Application preferences: language, appearance, display currency and the price-comparison site you selected;
  • Copies of the public data files described in Section 4, stored in the app's private storage.

The developer cannot access this information. PC Advisor does not operate a cloud account or synchronization service.

4. Data Updates

To keep its information up to date, PC Advisor connects only to devgroupeapp.github.io (hosted by GitHub Pages), at most about once a day, to download public data files and their digital signatures:

  • software requirements data (what popular applications need, with their official sources);
  • the parts catalog (component specifications from the makers' official pages, official launch prices where published, currency reference rates, and their sources and dates).

The app verifies the signature and contents of each file before using it.

  • The requests contain no personal data, no questionnaire answers, no saved recommendations or builds, no settings and no identifiers. Every user downloads the same public files.
  • As with any internet request, GitHub's servers receive technical connection information such as your IP address and basic request details. This is handled by GitHub under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The developer does not receive or have access to these server logs.
  • All connections use encrypted HTTPS. If you are offline, the app continues to work with the data already on your device.

5. Links to Other Websites

Some screens offer links to other websites:

  • the official pages of software publishers and component makers, which are the sources of the requirements and specifications shown in the app;
  • price-comparison websites in the Deals tab (for example GamingParts.ma in Morocco, PCPartPicker in supported countries, or Google Shopping elsewhere).

The app never opens these links by itself. A link opens in your browser only after you tap it and confirm a message that names the website. A price-comparison link contains only the name of the part you are looking for. Links contain no tracking or affiliate codes, and the developer receives no payment or commission from these websites.

To suggest a price-comparison website, the app reads your phone's region setting on your device. This setting is not sent anywhere, and you can choose another website in the app.

Once a website is open, that website's own privacy policy applies.

6. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration or sign-in, and no request for your name, email address or phone number;
  • No advertising and no advertising SDK. The app does not use the advertising identifier;
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools;
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access. The only permission requested is internet access, used for the data updates in Section 4.

7. Sharing and Disclosure

PC Advisor does not sell, share or transmit the information you enter into the app to anyone.

The app has no affiliate links and no feature that sends your recommendations, builds or answers to another person or service.

8. Android Device Backup

The application permits Android's system backup mechanisms. Depending on Android, your device manufacturer, your Google Account or other backup provider, and your device settings, your saved recommendations, saved builds and preferences may be included in a device backup so they can be restored on a new device. The downloaded data files are always excluded from backups and device-to-device transfers.

PC Advisor does not control Android's system-level backup service. To manage backups, use the backup settings of your Android device.

9. Data Deletion

You can delete your information at any time:

  • Delete saved recommendations and saved builds using the deletion controls in PC Advisor;
  • Change or reset your preferences in Settings;
  • Clear the app's data from your Android settings, or uninstall the app, which removes everything the app stored on the device.

Deletion from a device does not necessarily remove copies that may already exist in an operating-system backup. Such copies are governed by your device and backup-provider settings.

10. Third-Party Names

PC Advisor references software products (such as Adobe Premiere Pro or Blender), component makers and their products (such as AMD, Intel or NVIDIA) and price-comparison websites to identify them. These names belong to their respective owners. PC Advisor is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

11. Children's Privacy

PC Advisor is not designed specifically for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

12. Security

Your information is processed locally, and there is no PC Advisor cloud account containing your history. Data files are downloaded only over HTTPS from the developer's website and are checked with a digital signature before use.

You are responsible for protecting access to your Android device and for reviewing your device and backup security settings.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The date at the top of this page shows the current version. If advertising, analytics or any other data collection were ever added, this policy would be updated before that version is released.
