PC Advisor

Privacy Policy

How PC Advisor handles information and protects your privacy.

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Overview

PC Advisor is an Android application that helps you explore computer hardware configurations based on information you provide during the consultation.

The consultation and recommendation process runs entirely on your device. PC Advisor does not transmit your consultation selections, saved recommendations, or questionnaire answers to PC Advisor servers or to any third party.

In short: no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking. Your answers and saved recommendations stay on your device. The only network connection the app makes is to download a public file of software hardware requirements, and no personal data is sent with it.

2. Information You Enter

During a consultation, you may select information such as:

  • Device type (laptop or desktop) and intended use;
  • Workloads and software applications;
  • Gaming preferences, where applicable;
  • Budget range;
  • Hardware priorities;
  • Multitasking preferences; and
  • Other choices required by the recommendation questionnaire.

These selections are used only by the on-device recommendation engine to generate a PC configuration recommendation.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

PC Advisor uses local storage for application functionality:

  • Saved recommendations, together with the questionnaire requirements used to produce them, stored in the app's private local database;
  • Application preferences, such as language and appearance settings;
  • A copy of the public software requirements data file described in Section 4, stored in the app's private storage.

The developer cannot access this information. PC Advisor does not operate a cloud account or synchronization service.

4. Software Requirements Data Updates

To keep software hardware requirements up to date, PC Advisor connects only to devgroupeapp.github.io (hosted by GitHub Pages), at most about once a day, to download a public requirements data file and its digital signature. The app verifies the signature before using the file.

  • The request contains no personal data, no questionnaire answers, no saved recommendations and no identifiers. Every user downloads the same public file.
  • As with any internet request, GitHub's servers receive technical connection information such as your IP address and basic request details. This is handled by GitHub under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The developer does not receive or have access to these server logs.
  • All connections use encrypted HTTPS. If you are offline, the app continues to work with the data already on your device.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration or sign-in, and no request for your name, email address or phone number;
  • No advertising and no advertising SDK. The app does not use the advertising identifier;
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools;
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access. The only permission requested is internet access.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

PC Advisor does not sell, share or transmit the information you enter into the app to anyone.

The application does not provide retailer links, affiliate links, or other external sharing mechanisms for your consultation data.

7. Android Device Backup

The application permits Android's system backup mechanisms. Depending on Android, your device manufacturer, your Google Account or other backup provider, and your device settings, your saved recommendations and preferences may be included in a device backup so they can be restored on a new device. The downloaded requirements data file is excluded from backups.

PC Advisor does not control Android's system-level backup service. To manage backups, use the backup settings of your Android device.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete your information at any time:

  • Delete saved recommendations using the deletion controls in PC Advisor;
  • Change or reset language and appearance preferences;
  • Clear the app's data from your Android settings, or uninstall the app, which removes everything the app stored on the device.

Deletion from a device does not necessarily remove copies that may already exist in an operating-system backup. Such copies are governed by your device and backup-provider settings.

9. Third-Party Software Names

PC Advisor references software products such as Adobe Premiere Pro or Blender to match applications with hardware requirements. These names are used for identification only and belong to their respective owners. PC Advisor is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

10. Children's Privacy

PC Advisor is not designed specifically for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

11. Security

Consultation information is processed locally, and there is no PC Advisor cloud account containing your history. Requirements data is downloaded only over HTTPS from the developer's website and is checked with a digital signature before use.

You are responsible for protecting access to your Android device and for reviewing your device and backup security settings.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The date at the top of this page shows the current version. If advertising, analytics or any other data collection were ever added, this policy would be updated before that version is released.
